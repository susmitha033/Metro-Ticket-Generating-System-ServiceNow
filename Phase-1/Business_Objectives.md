# Business Objectives

## Objective

The Metro Ticket Generating System is designed to simplify metro ticket booking by providing a digital platform in ServiceNow. The system enables passengers to book tickets easily, calculates fares automatically, and generates QR code-based digital tickets for a faster, safer, and more convenient travel experience.

## Business Goals

### 1. Improve Passenger Experience
- Provide a simple and user-friendly ticket booking process.
- Reduce waiting time at metro ticket counters.
- Generate instant QR code-based digital tickets.

### 2. Increase Operational Efficiency
- Automate the ticket booking and fare calculation process.
- Reduce manual work and minimize human errors.
- Improve the overall efficiency of metro ticket management.

### 3. Encourage Digital Payments
- Support secure cashless payment methods.
- Promote faster and more convenient digital transactions.

### 4. Improve Data Management
- Store ticket booking details digitally.
- Generate reports for bookings and passenger statistics.
- Support better decision-making through accurate data.

### 5. Support Sustainable Transportation
- Reduce the use of paper tickets.
- Encourage an eco-friendly and digital ticketing system.
